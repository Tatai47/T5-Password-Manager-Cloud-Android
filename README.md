> **T5 Password Manager** is designed to keep your digital life secure on the go. By utilizing industry-standard local encryption, autofill capabilities, and a distraction-free UI, it ensures your sensitive data is always protected and easily accessible.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* 🔐 **Bank-Grade Encryption:** Local AES-256 encryption ensures your vault is impenetrable.
* ⚡ **Fully Safe by T5:** This app is totally maintainded by T5 so your data is safe here.
* 👆 **Password Authentication:** Quick, secure access using password Unlock.
* 🎲 **Smart Password Generator:** Create strong, complex, and unique passwords on the fly.
* 📴 **Offline-First Architecture:** Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable sync.
