# T5 Password Manager - Android 📱

T5 Password Manager is a secure, lightweight, and intuitive password management application designed specifically for Android devices. Safely store, generate, and autofill your passwords on the go.

## Features
* **Secure Encryption:** Industry-standard local encryption to keep your vault safe.
* **Autofill Service:** Seamlessly log into your favorite apps and websites.
* **Biometric Login:** Quick access using fingerprint or face unlock.
* **Password Generator:** Create strong, unique passwords instantly.
* **Offline First:** Your data stays on your device unless you choose to sync.

## Prerequisites
* Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher.
* [Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio) (if building from source).
